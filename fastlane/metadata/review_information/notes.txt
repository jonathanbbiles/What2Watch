Moodie helps you decide what to watch tonight. Pick how you want to feel, tap Shuffle, and Moodie suggests one title with a one-line reason why. "Watch" hands off to the streaming service that carries it.

THIRD-PARTY CONTENT (relevant to the earlier 4.1(a) review)
- Every poster, synopsis and rating comes from TMDB (The Movie Database) via its public API, under terms that permit display in an app like this. Nothing is scraped; no Wikipedia code remains. Moodie bundles no media - images load from TMDB's own CDN at display time.
- Where-to-watch data comes from TMDB's /watch/providers endpoint (powered by JustWatch).
- Required credits are shown in the app: "This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB." and "Where-to-watch data provided by JustWatch." (You screen and each pick).
- No provider logos or brand marks in our UI. Service names appear as plain text only: in the "Your services" picker (the user's own subscriptions) and on the pick's Watch button ("Watch on Netflix"), which reflects TMDB/JustWatch data for that title. Moodie is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any streaming service.
- Store screenshots are rendered from the shipping build with the app's own generated card art; no studio key art or provider logos.

WATCH BUTTON
Tapping Watch opens the streaming service's own app if it is installed (via its URL scheme, declared in LSApplicationQueriesSchemes); otherwise it opens the service's website, or the title's JustWatch page, in the system browser (SFSafariViewController). Moodie never plays video itself.

NETWORK USE
Moodie calls only the TMDB API: discover/search for candidate titles, details for the title on screen, and its watch providers. Requests carry only the lookup itself - no user data, identifiers or analytics. Moods, shuffling, Together, Saved and Seen are computed on device. Offline, posters fall back to Moodie's gradient card art and everything else keeps working.

ACCOUNT / PURCHASES
- No account, login or sign-up. Fully usable on first launch; nothing is gated.
- The app is FREE. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no paid tier mentioned anywhere.
- No demo credentials needed.

WHAT A REVIEWER WILL SEE
1. Welcome - animated Moodie mascot, "Let's go".
2. Your services - pick the streaming services you use (local preference; none preselected; searchable list of any TMDB provider).
3. Seen it? - an optional, skippable grid of popular titles to mark as already seen so they are never suggested; searchable, infinite scroll.
4. Shuffle (Home) - the mood grid; tap one or more moods, then "Shuffle my pick".
5. Your pick - poster, why it matched, synopsis, rating, IMDb link, and the Watch / Shuffle again / Not for me / Save buttons. Fits one screen.
6. Together - pass-the-phone group picking with "Popcorn Pals": each pal taps their moods in turn and Moodie shows the one title with the best overlap. Entirely on device; no accounts, invites or server.
7. Saved - watch-later grid.
8. You - your services, pals, seen list, an "include titles I've seen" toggle, Privacy Policy and Support links, and the TMDB / JustWatch attributions.

NATIVE FEATURES
Haptic feedback on mood taps, shuffles and reveals; native iOS Share sheet; on-device storage (Capacitor Preferences) for services, seen list, ratings and saved titles; App Launcher for opening streaming apps; in-app system browser for websites.

PRIVACY
No account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs beyond Capacitor plugins. Privacy policy: https://jonathanscribbles.com/moodie#privacy
